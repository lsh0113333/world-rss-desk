Markets rally as rates fall

By Jane Doe

Stock markets around the world rallied on Friday after the central bank signalled that interest rates would fall faster than expected, lifting technology and property shares.

Investors react

Analysts said the move surprised many investors, who had priced in a slower pace of cuts. Bond yields dropped sharply in early trading.

"This changes the outlook for the rest of the year," one strategist said.

  • Tech shares up 4%
  • Property shares up 2.5%

Currency markets were also volatile, with the dollar weakening against most major currencies as traders adjusted their expectations for the months ahead.
